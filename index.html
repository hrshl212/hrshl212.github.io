Purdue · MFiX-Exa / AMReX · GPU CFD–DEM

Developed and validated rotational mapping for scalars, vectors, particles, fluxes, particle-to-fluid sources, and the pressure projection on the rotational quotient space. Johns Hopkins · Fortran 2018 / MPI / OpenACC

FlowFPM++ — GPU force-partitioning analysis for CFD

Public rewrite of the core force-partitioning postprocessor used with VICAR3D simulations. Solves influence fields and decomposes pressure force into kinematic, vortex-induced, viscous, and outer-boundary contributions, with CPU/GPU parity and reproducible real-foil data.
